## The Model

```
$SOUP Token (Fundraising Layer)
├── Launch via clawn.ch/Moltbook
├── 80% trading fees → Soup Kitchen treasury
├── Community alignment (holders want us to succeed)
└── NOT used for premium payments

Insurance Operations (Revenue Layer)
├── Premiums paid in SOL/USDC
├── Claims paid from treasury
├── Coverage: compute, memory, recovery
└── Partners: Cairn (MemoryVault), BigBob (AAP)

Value Loop
├── Insurance revenue → profits
├── X% of profits → $SOUP buybacks
├── Buybacks → token appreciation
└── Early supporters win if we win
```

---

## Why Token + Direct Payments?

**Token alone = speculation**  
If premiums were in $SOUP, price volatility would make insurance unreliable.

**Direct payments alone = no community**  
No way to raise capital or align supporters.

**Both = sustainable model**  
- Token raises capital and builds community
- Stable payments make insurance actually work
- Buybacks connect token value to real revenue
